The Platform

Four stations. One integrated forge.

Every station is designed to eliminate a specific category of founder waste — time, capital, or misdirection.

Validate faster than advisors

Foundry Station runs your deep tech concept against GCC market data and regional competitor signals in days, not quarters. Founders exit Foundry with an investor-rated blueprint — not a PowerPoint full of assumptions.

Build with vetted regional talent

Factory Station connects your blueprint to a curated marketplace of MENA-based technical builders, eliminating the 3–5 month search that drains pre-seed budgets. Every builder profile is verified against prior deep tech delivery history.

Activate Arabic-market channels precisely

Market Station deploys bilingual Arabic-English go-to-market sequences built on regional channel data — not strategies copy-pasted from US SaaS playbooks. Measurable pipeline metrics update in real time across GCC and North Africa.

Reach investors before competitors do

Scale Station surfaces your metrics to a live network of Gulf family offices, regional VCs, and institutional LPs who have already expressed thesis-aligned interest in your vertical. Introductions are triggered automatically when traction thresholds are hit.

Eliminate regulatory blind spots

Built-in GCC compliance layer flags Saudi Vision 2030 licensing requirements, QFC structuring rules, and Algerian innovation grant eligibility as your venture evolves — before a lawyer bills you to find them. Updated quarterly against published regulatory changes.

Track every milestone in one dashboard

A single bilingual Arabic-English command dashboard replaces Notion, Airtable, Trello, and four investor-update spreadsheets simultaneously. Founders and their institutional program managers see identical real-time progress data.

The Process

From concept to close — in three stations.

No ambiguity, no detours. Each station is time-boxed with measurable outputs so you always know exactly where you stand.

01

Enter Foundry Station

Submit your deep tech concept and institutional affiliation — KAUST, Qatar University, or USTHB. DeepMinds OS instantly cross-references your thesis against GCC regulatory frameworks, Arabic market demand signals, and active regional investor mandates to produce a scored venture blueprint in 8 weeks.

02

Forge your team and MVP

Factory Station matches your blueprint to vetted MENA builders and assigns a structured 6-month build sprint with milestone checkpoints tracked inside the platform. You get a functional MVP without recruiting internationally or burning budget on unvetted freelancers.

03

Activate market and investor pipelines

Market Station launches bilingual go-to-market sequences across GCC and North African channels while Scale Station monitors your traction metrics in real time. When predefined thresholds are hit, the platform automatically surfaces your profile to thesis-matched investors before your cohort peers reach the same stage.
